Our Design Process

When you work with Plankraft as your architect in Calicut, you follow a structured, collaborative process:

  • Consultation: We begin with a detailed discussion about your requirements, budget, site conditions, and aesthetic preferences.
  • Concept Design: Our team develops initial design concepts with floor plans, elevations, and 3D visualisations for your review.
  • Design Development: Based on your feedback, we refine the design, finalise materials, and prepare detailed construction drawings.
